# Before commerce launch

- Confirm proposed variant names, integer prices, currency, inventory and product dimensions.
- Supply real packaging/product photos and approved social image.
- Confirm exact use, drying and safety/supervision instructions, respecting the packaging's age 8+ guidance.
- Confirm delivery coverage/costs/timing, returns, contact, social accounts and applicable privacy/commerce policies.
- Connect and test a server-side payment provider and signed webhook/order workflow.
- Configure a canonical HTTPS origin, rebuild and inspect sitemap/metadata.
- Review on physical iOS/Android devices, keyboard and screen reader, and perform host-specific performance testing. Chromium software-rendered testing cannot establish every device's GPU performance.
- Customer gallery content is optional; keep current items clearly labelled studio samples unless permitted customer photos are supplied.
