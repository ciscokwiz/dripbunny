# Drip Bunny design system

Reference: the supplied four-colour packaging. Rounded oversized head, upright oval ears, compact torso, short limbs; glossy white veins over saturated poured paint. This procedural model is an interpretation, not an exact product replica.

Creative direction: a cream-coloured paint studio with raspberry typography, oversized Fredoka display headings, Nunito Sans body text, pill controls, asymmetric stickers and liquid shapes. Ruby, blue, green and gold themes follow the supplied palette. Pale surfaces and generous space balance saturated artwork.

Primary visual hierarchy: playful headline → interactive bunny → clear shopping CTA. Product images and business facts are configuration-driven. Sample art is labelled; no fictional customer content or prices. Motion uses short spring entrances and subtle reveals, with native navigation and reduced-motion alternatives.
